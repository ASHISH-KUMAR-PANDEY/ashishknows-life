STAGE / Growth & discovery

Launching vertical micro-dramas.

I helped launch V-Series as a distinct streaming category and worked on getting more viewers from discovering it to pressing play.

5% → to 18%Share of total content starts

The product question

How do you introduce a short, vertical format inside a service that already has an established viewing experience? Viewers needed to find V-Series, understand what made it different, and have an easy way to try it.

What I worked on

I helped define the positioning and product experience at launch. After launch, I worked on discovery and adoption, including the surfaces where viewers encounter vertical stories and the steps between seeing one and watching it.

What changed

V-Series grew from 5% to 18% of total content starts as we improved discovery and adoption. That metric measures how much of all viewing starts the new format accounted for.
