STAGE / AI engagement

Characters beyond the credits.

I helped build an AI platform that let viewers talk to characters from the shows they watch.

30K+Weekly active users

The product question

Could characters viewers already knew give them a reason to come back between episodes? The first experience was a conversation with the character, in persona.

What I worked on

I worked on making the path from discovery into chat clearer, including how viewers were routed into a conversation. I also built long-term memory so a character could retain context and pick up a conversation over time.

What we saw

The experience reached more than 30,000 active users in a week. We also observed more title watch-through among viewers who chatted than among non-exposed users. That comparison describes how those groups behaved.
